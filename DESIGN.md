## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the synthesis of senior executive business management with advanced data analytics and artificial intelligence leadership. The tone is authoritative, analytical, and forward-looking—conveying institutional competence alongside high-velocity technological agility. 

Drawing from modern high-contrast corporate minimalism with subtle technical accents, the interface eschews gimmicks in favor of razor-sharp alignment, structured data density, and clear typographic hierarchy. The aesthetic mimics high-end executive command centers and strategic briefings: refined dark navy foundations, controlled electric indigo visual anchors, and pinpoint cyan highlights that signal algorithmic precision and intelligence.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive dark canvas anchored by deep navy, layered slate surfaces, and high-visibility intelligence signals.

- **Canvas & Structural Neutrals:** `#0B192C` serves as the root canvas. Elevated card containers and side panels utilize slate-shifted midnight tones (`#0F2238` and `#132B47`), framed by subtle border delineators (`#1E3A5F`).
- **Primary Accent (`#4F46E5`):** Electric indigo serves as the core authority driver, used for key executive roles, active navigational indicators, structural badges, and primary calls to action.
- **Secondary Accent (`#06B6D4`):** Cyan/teal acts strictly as an intelligence metric accent—highlighting AI, algorithmic KPIs, percentage increases, and technical credentials.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Text relies on pure high-legibility slate whites (`#F8FAFC`) for primary titles, cool neutral slates (`#94A3B8`) for body prose and analytical narrative, and muted steel (`#64748B`) for metadata and timestamps.

## Typography

Typography pairs the structural refinement of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles and metrics with the functional clarity of Inter for dense career timelines and technical narratives.

- **Scale & Weight:** Headings use heavy weights (`700`–`800`) with tightened tracking to project boardroom gravity. Body copy relies on regular weights (`400`) at optimal reading heights (`1.5`–`1.75rem`) to maintain effortless scanning across dense career achievements.
- **Data & Metric Displays:** Numerical metrics, ROI indicators, and scale statistics use `metric-display`, rendering numbers with tabular spacing to ensure crisp comparative alignment.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Categories, dates, and domain markers utilize uppercase `label-sm` with widened letter-spacing (`0.05em`) to balance against deep background values.

## Layout & Spacing

The system is engineered on an asymmetric 12-column responsive grid structured specifically for leadership profiles:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 4-column persistent sidebar (executive summary, verified credentials, key impact metrics, core competencies) coupled with an 8-column main pane (leadership experience timeline, board governance, AI initiatives, publications).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Collapses into an integrated 8-column layout with impact metrics repositioned directly under the header.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked vertical hierarchy. Gutters step down from `1.5rem` to `1rem`, and canvas margins scale from `2.5rem` to `1.25rem`.

Spacing adheres strictly to vertical rhythm tiers. Gaps between distinct career roles leverage `space-xl`, while internal card padding for roles and metric displays uses `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering combined with low-contrast structural borders, avoiding diffuse drop shadows in favor of a crisp, technical aesthetic:

- **Base Layer:** Root canvas `#0B192C`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#0F2238` with a 1px continuous border of `#1E3A5F`. No drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover Cards & Metric Badges):** `#132B47` with a 1px border shift to `#4F46E5` (at 40% alpha) and an ambient outer glow: `0 0 20px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Overlays / Floating Panels):** `#0B192C` at 90% opacity paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a crisp outer border of `#334155`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a balanced roundedness standard (`0.5rem` / `rounded-md`) to reconcile contemporary UI softness with architectural precision:

- **Cards & Profile Modules:** Base radius of `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`). Major container groupings use `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pills & Data Badges:** High-density skill chips, metric tags, and operational status indicators use full-radius pill silhouettes to contrast against rectangular card geometry.
- **Timeline Nodes:** Exact circular nodes (`rounded-full`) connected by precise 2px continuous vertical axes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Executive Action (Export Briefing / Contact):** Solid `#4F46E5` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, with subtle hover lift and `#6366F1` highlight.
- **Secondary Action (View Patent / Case Study):** Transparent background with a 1px `#1E3A5F` border, `#F8FAFC` text, shifting to `#0F2238` surface on hover.

### Metric Badges
- High-visibility statistical cards featuring large `metric-display` values colored in `#06B6D4` paired with an uppercase `label-sm` subtitle in `#94A3B8`. Encased in an elevated card with subtle border delimitation.

### Skills & Competency Chips
- **AI & Analytics Tags:** Pill-shaped, background `#06B6D4` at 10% opacity, text `#06B6D4`, and a 1px border of `#06B6D4` at 25% opacity.
- **Executive Leadership Tags:** Pill-shaped, background `#4F46E5` at 10% opacity, text `#A5B4FC`, and a 1px border of `#4F46E5` at 25% opacity.

### Career Timeline
- Continuous 2px vertical spine in `#1E3A5F`. 
- **Timeline Nodes:** Concentric ring nodes featuring an outer 12px ring in `#0F2238` with an inner 6px solid `#4F46E5` pip for historical roles, shifting to `#06B6D4` with pulse effect for the active current role.
- **Role Entries:** Company, title, and date in `headline-md`, followed by bulleted achievements highlighted by bold impact prefixes.

### Form Inputs & Search Filters
- Inputs use `#0F2238` background, 1px `#1E3A5F` border, `#F8FAFC` input text, and focus outlines anchored by `#4F46E5` with `0 0 0 2px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.2)`.
